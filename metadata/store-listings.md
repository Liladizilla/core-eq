## Google Play

**App name:** CORE-EQ: Personal Audio  
**Short description:** A precision 10-band equalizer interface with on-device DSP test playback.  
**Full description:**

CORE-EQ is a hardware-inspired audio control deck built around a 10-band graphic equalizer and a native digital signal processing engine.

Explore frequency shaping from 32 Hz to 16 kHz, adjust output level, and experiment with bass boost, limiter, bypass, and mid/side spatial widening using the built-in generated test tone.

**Current build scope:** the available Android build processes CORE-EQ's generated 440 Hz test tone. It does not currently equalize audio from Spotify or other apps, capture microphone input, or provide universal system-wide audio processing. Device compatibility and audio routing can vary.

**Privacy:** audio processing is designed to run on-device. Do not publish a privacy-policy URL until a real policy page is available and reviewed against the final app behavior.

**Release status:** internal/device testing build. Not yet a public Play Store release.
